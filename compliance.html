IMMEDIATE PROGRAMME DECISION

Do not launch referral-linked rewards for registered architects without written legal advice.

The Architects (Professional Conduct) Regulations, 1989 state that an architect shall not give or take discounts, commissions, gifts or other inducements for introducing clients or work. A points programme offering trips, gadgets or benefits based on referrals may create a direct professional-conduct risk.

Pause architect reward eligibilityCapture COA registration statusObtain specialist written opinionRedesign recognition if required
Council of Architecture regulation ↗

SAFER INTERIM MODEL

Separate recognition from referral consideration.

Until counsel approves the structure, registered architects should receive no per-lead, per-conversion or points-based benefit. Educational events or professional recognition must also be reviewed so they cannot operate as an inducement.

Disclosure alone may not cure an express professional prohibition.

REGULATORY CONTROL MAP

What the business must take care of.

This is an operational checklist, not a substitute for advice on the final programme terms.

01CRITICAL

ARCHITECTS ACT + PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT

Professional ethics and referral inducements

  • Identify registered architects separately from other partner types.
  • Do not release commission, gifts, trips, gadgets or points tied to introductions until legal review.
  • Keep the professional-ethics decision in the programme terms and reward engine.

02HIGH

DPDP ACT 2023 + DPDP RULES 2025

Personal data and privacy governance

  • Give a clear notice stating every purpose before collecting partner or customer data.
  • Collect only what is necessary; provide access, correction, erasure, withdrawal and grievance routes.
  • Encrypt or mask personal data, restrict access, monitor activity, back up records and bind vendors contractually.
  • Design now for the main operational provisions scheduled after the notified 18-month transition.

03HIGH

CONSENT + PURPOSE LIMITATION

Customer confirmation is not marketing permission

  • Record separate consent for referral verification and for promotional communication.
  • Store the notice version, purpose, timestamp, channel, response and withdrawal history.
  • Never pre-tick consent or bundle optional marketing into mandatory verification.
  • Restrict the programme to adults unless verifiable parental-consent controls are implemented.

SMS, calls and WhatsApp communications

  • Register the business/principal entity, headers, consent and content templates where required for SMS or calls.
  • Use DLT-compliant routes and approved, whitelisted message links.
  • Maintain opt-in and revocation evidence; honour customer preferences and opt-outs.
  • Keep service verification messages separate from marketing campaigns.

IT ACT + CERT-IN DIRECTIONS

Cybersecurity and incident response

  • Prepare a six-hour CERT-In reporting playbook for covered cyber incidents.
  • Complete independent security testing before production and after material changes.

06MEDIUM

CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT + DARK PATTERNS

Fair programme terms and user experience

  • State point value, eligibility, stock, expiry, cooling, cancellation and redemption rules before enrolment.
  • Do not advertise guaranteed rewards when inventory or approval conditions apply.
  • Avoid hidden expiry, forced consent, confirm-shaming, trick wording, false urgency and difficult cancellation.
  • Publish grievance contact details and a resolution path.
Owner · Legal + Customer serviceOfficial guidelines ↗
07HIGH

INCOME-TAX ACT §194R + GST

Reward valuation, TDS and accounting

  • Have the CA classify every reward and determine whether section 194R applies.
  • Section 194R can apply to business/professional benefits in cash or kind; the statutory threshold and exceptions must be checked each financial year.
  • Capture PAN, recipient category, fair value, release date, TDS status and certificates where legally required.
  • Have the CA document GST treatment for discounts, vouchers, third-party rewards and cancellations.
Owner · Chartered AccountantSection 194R ↗
08MEDIUM

RBI PPI / PAYMENT-SYSTEM BOUNDARY

Keep loyalty points closed and non-cash

  • Points should be non-transferable, not withdrawable as cash and governed by clear expiry and reversal rules.
  • Do not allow peer-to-peer transfer, merchant-wide spending or cash conversion without payment-law review.
  • Use an RBI-regulated payment provider for any paid transaction; never store raw card credentials.
  • Review third-party gift cards and travel rewards separately.
Owner · Finance + Banking counselRBI PPI directions ↗
09MEDIUM

AADHAAR ACT + UIDAI VERIFICATION RULES

Use identity data proportionately

  • Do not make Aadhaar mandatory for an ordinary loyalty programme without a lawful basis.
  • Prefer mobile OTP and approved professional/business identifiers.
  • If Aadhaar offline verification is used, follow UIDAI’s consent and OVSE requirements and avoid collecting or storing the Aadhaar number or biometrics.
  • Mask identifiers everywhere outside authorised verification workflows.

10MEDIUM

CONTRACT ACT + IT ACT + EVIDENCE LAW

Terms, acceptance and electronic evidence

  • Use versioned partner terms with clear acceptance, eligibility, ownership, dispute and termination clauses.
  • Capture electronic acceptance, OTP events, message delivery, timestamps and the exact terms shown.
  • Maintain reliable exports and evidence certificates/processes for electronic records if a dispute reaches formal proceedings.
  • Prohibit account sharing, false claims, manipulation and collusion expressly.

EMPLOYEE + VENDOR GOVERNANCE

Access, confidentiality and accountability

  • Give employees only the modules and actions their work requires.
  • Use individual accounts, strong authentication, immediate offboarding and periodic access reviews.
  • Record every ownership override, points adjustment, export and reward approval.
  • Sign confidentiality, data-processing, security, breach-support and deletion clauses with employees and vendors.

12HIGH

RETENTION + RIGHTS + BREACH RESPONSE

Keep evidence without keeping everything forever

  • Approve a data schedule for lead, referral, consent, invoice, reward, tax, security and dispute records.
  • Erase or anonymise data when purpose and legal retention end; preserve active disputes and statutory records.
  • Provide an accessible grievance channel and track corrections, withdrawals and deletion requests.
  • Maintain breach notifications, investigation evidence and remediation records.

13CRITICAL

ANTI-BRIBERY + PUBLIC-OFFICIAL CONTROLS

Block rewards where influence creates corruption risk

  • Identify government, local-authority, public-sector and procurement-linked participants before reward eligibility.
  • Default-block gifts, trips, gadgets, vouchers and points for public officials or anyone influencing public procurement until specialist approval.
  • Require conflict declarations, a gift register and documented approval for exceptional cases.
  • Do not route a blocked benefit through a relative, nominee, employee or third-party vendor.

COPYRIGHT + TRADE MARKS + MEDIA RIGHTS

Use only authorised brand and reward media

  • Do not treat public posting as permission; obtain written confirmation for client, partner and vendor assets.
  • Record source, owner, licence scope, expiry and approval for uploaded media.
  • Obtain consent for names, photographs and testimonials and maintain a takedown process.
